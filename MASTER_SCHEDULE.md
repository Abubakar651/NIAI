## ⚖️ End-of-Day Rollover & Debt Bank System
Every day at 10:00 PM, an audit is performed:
$$\text{Actual Hours} = \text{LEAR (target: 6.0h)} + \text{LAW (target: 2.0h)} + \text{JOB (target: 1.0h)}$$

* **Balanced (9.0h)**: All hours logged to Jira, debt remains 0.
* **Surplus (> 9.0h)**: Extra hours logged, celebrated.
* **Deficit (< 9.0h)**:
  $$\text{Deficit} = 9.0 - \text{Actual Hours}$$
  The deficit rolls over to the next day's schedule or Saturday catch-up session. It remains tracked until cleared.
