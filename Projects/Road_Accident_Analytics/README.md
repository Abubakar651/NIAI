# Road Accident Analytics & Dashboard

**Author:** Abubakar Iqbal  
**Course:** NAVTTC / NIAI (NETSOL Technologies)  
**Dataset:** 5,000 incident records (2024–2025)

---

## Project Overview
This project analyzes 5,000 road accident records to identify high-risk conditions, accident hotspots, severity drivers, and demographic risk factors, culminating in an interactive Microsoft Excel executive dashboard.

---

## Workbook Structure (`Road_Accident_Dataset(completed).xlsx`)
1. **`Dashboard`**: Interactive one-page dashboard featuring:
   - 5 KPI summary cards: Total Accidents, Total Casualties, Fatal Accidents, Serious Accidents, Slight Accidents.
   - 5 visual charts: Monthly accident trends, severity distribution, accident frequency by road type, light & weather conditions, and vehicle involvement.
   - 4 dynamic slicers: Accident Severity, Weather Condition, Road Type, Urban vs Rural.
2. **`Insights_Recommendations`**: Data-backed findings and safety recommendations.
3. **`Accident_Data`**: Cleaned and validated dataset with normalized attributes.
4. **`Pivot_Calculations`**: Calculated PivotTables and aggregation models driving dashboard visuals.
5. **`Data_Dictionary`**: Variable descriptions, types, and allowed values.

---

## Key Data Insights
1. **Weather & Light Conditions**: Over 68% of fatal accidents occur during darkness or low-light conditions on wet/unlit rural highways.
2. **Road Type Risk**: Single carriageway rural roads account for the largest proportion of fatal incidents due to higher speed differentials and overtaking maneuvers.
3. **Vehicle & Driver Profiles**: Young drivers (aged 18–25) and motorized two-wheelers display disproportionately higher casualty-to-accident ratios.

---

## Practical Safety Recommendations
1. **Rural Road Lighting & Signage**: Prioritize LED street illumination and reflective chevron markers on high-frequency rural curves.
2. **Targeted Speed Enforcement**: Deploy automated speed cameras along identified single-carriageway accident corridors.
3. **Vulnerable Road User Protection**: Implement segregated two-wheeler lanes and strict mandatory helmet enforcement in high-density zones.
